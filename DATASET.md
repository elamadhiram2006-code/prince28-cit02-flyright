

\# Dataset: FlyRight



Explains air passenger rights in India (cancellation, delay, denied boarding, baggage, assistance) and what compensation applies, citing DGCA rules.



\- \*\*Start target:\*\* Passenger CARs + 2 airlines' conditions of carriage

\- \*\*Full target:\*\* + all relevant CARs, revisions and 4 airlines' terms (1,200+ pages)

\- \*\*Collected by:\*\* `scripts/fetch.py` as `examate-student-bot`: robots.txt respected, at most 4 requests at once, 0.5 s between requests to one site.



\## Where the documents come from



\- https://www.dgca.gov.in/

\- https://www.civilaviation.gov.in/

\- https://airsewa.gov.in/



\## Terms I follow



Public regulations. Cite CAR section and revision date.



\- Every answer links to the original document.
